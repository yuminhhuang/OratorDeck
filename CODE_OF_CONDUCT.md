# OratorDeck Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OratorDeck is an open-source project for people preparing presentations,
research talks, oral defenses, and presentation-video tooling. We want its
community spaces to be useful, welcoming, and safe for people of every
background and level of experience.

## Expected conduct

When participating in this project, please:

- be respectful, specific, and constructive;
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people;
- make room for questions, incomplete work, and different levels of expertise;
- respect privacy, copyright, attribution, and presentation materials that are
  not yours to redistribute;
- disclose when examples contain synthetic or generated media; and
- accept a maintainer's request to stop conduct that is disrupting the
  community.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- deliberate misgendering, doxxing, or publishing private information;
- sustained disruption, personal attacks, or bad-faith participation;
- submitting confidential slide decks, personal voice data, or other material
  without permission; and
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces where someone is representing OratorDeck or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For routine moderation requests, contact the repository maintainers. For a
private report, open a confidential report through
[GitHub's private reporting channel](https://github.com/yuminhhuang/OratorDeck/security/advisories/new).
Include links or screenshots when possible, but do not investigate or expose
other people in order to collect evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a private warning, limit
participation temporarily, or ban participation permanently. The response will
be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, and whether it continues after a
warning. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

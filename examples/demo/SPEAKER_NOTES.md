# OratorDeck Demo

## Slide 01 - Aligned Inputs

**Target time:** 0:25

OratorDeck begins with two inputs that already agree: a rendered slide and its
speaker notes. It preserves those **aligned assets** through speech generation,
captioning, annotation, and final video assembly.

## Slide 02 - Four Stages

**Target time:** 0:30

The workflow has four visible stages. We chunk the notes, generate the speech,
**align the anchors**, and render the final video. Each slide remains an
indivisible unit, making failures easier to diagnose and retry.

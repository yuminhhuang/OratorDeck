# OratorDeck Demo

## Slide 01 - Aligned Inputs

**Target time:** 0:13

OratorDeck begins with two inputs that already agree: **Speaker notes** and a
**Slide image**. It preserves those **aligned assets** through speech
generation, captioning, annotation, and final video assembly.

## Slide 02 - Four Stages

**Target time:** 0:15

The workflow has four visible stages. We **chunk notes**, **generate speech**,
**align the anchors**, and **render video**. Each slide remains an indivisible
unit, making failures easier to diagnose and retry.

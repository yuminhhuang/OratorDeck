# Speaker Notes

## Slide 01 - Opening

**Target time:** 0:30

Welcome to the presentation. We will begin with the **central question**,
explain why it matters, and then follow the evidence to a practical conclusion.

## Slide 02 - Approach

**Target time:** 0:45

The approach has three stages. First, define the decision clearly. Second,
collect the **relevant evidence**. Third, compare the alternatives under the
same assumptions so that the final recommendation remains transparent.
